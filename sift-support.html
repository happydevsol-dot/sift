Need help?

If you're having trouble with Sift, have a question, or want to report a problem, contact us by email.

Email: happy.dev.sol@gmail.com

Common support topics

We can help with:

  • Account and sign-in issues
  • Email, Google, or Sign in with Apple
  • Push notifications
  • Local news and location settings
  • Saved stories and preferences
  • English, Arabic, and Hebrew language support
  • Ads and privacy choices
  • Bug reports or unexpected behavior

Account deletion

If you have a Sift account, you can request account deletion directly from within the app through your account settings.

If you are unable to access your account or need help with deletion, contact us at happy.dev.sol@gmail.com .

Privacy

About Sift

Sift helps you compare how different news sources report the same story. It brings together coverage, wording differences, framing, claims, sources, timelines, and related context so you can get a clearer view of the news.
